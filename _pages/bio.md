## The short version

A native of Guadalajara, Mexico, I am a mathematician trained in analysis and number theory.
My research has included random matrix theory and analytic number theory, 
but has shifted towards the foundations of analysis, probability, and computation 
—with a growing interest in applications to artificial intelligence.
I am faculty at UT San Antonio and have started consulting for the AI industry.


## Research and explorations: what I enjoy thinking about

If there is a single thread running through my interests, it is a fondness for
**broad, general patterns** —the structures that recur across very different
subjects once you squint.

As an analyst, some of the fundamental questions I ponder pertain to
measure and integration, probability and information, and the theory of
*real-valued* and *continuous* logics.
Continuous logic is a network connecting analysis with model theory;
real-valued logic, in particular, underlies contemporary AI.
I know my way around computer code: a skill that serves me
as researcher, teacher, and AI consultant of late.

I also very much continue to enjoy recreational problem-solving.
Nothing I love more than a problem that forces you to understand
something you know a little better, to learn something new
to solve it, or to connect a little question to bigger ones.


## Teaching: what it means to me

As much as I enjoy thinking about and learning mathematics,
the human and cultural aspects of the discipline are just as important.
I take my role as educator to heart and like to push at curricular boundaries,
try new pedagogical approaches, and hold students to high standards they can meet. 
I will mentor any students having a sincere desire to learn 
and the discipline and drive to go along with the hunger for knowledge
—regardless of ethnic and socioeconomic background,
gender identity, or any other personal and life circumstances.
Nevertheless, opportunities to work with students from
non-traditional, underprivileged or similar backgrounds to mine are special.
My [acknowledgments](/thanks/) give context to these values
I hold as a teacher.

Over the years, I have mentored hundreds of students entering math competitions
as well as UTSA’s Putnam team for the better part of two decades.
I do spend the occasional hour or two figuring out an interesting
competition problem with my students —or by myself.
