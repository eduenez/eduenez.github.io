Associate Professor of Mathematics at [The University of Texas at San Antonio](https://www.utsa.edu), and research consultant at [Gibran.ai](https://gibran.ai).

{{ site.data.cv.research_interests.summary | markdownify }}

Recent and current research is on foundations of analysis from the perspective of real-valued and other continuous logics, including:

- Measure and integration.
- Probability and information theory.
- Real-valued computability: classical/deterministic and randomized/quantum.
- Statistical/machine learning.
